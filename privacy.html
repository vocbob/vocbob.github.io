Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · 한국어

CATCHI ("CATCHI", "we", "us") is a mobile app that combines a daily fortune with a virtual pixel cat. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. CATCHI has no user accounts and does not run its own servers that store your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following information is saved in the app's local storage on your device. We do not receive or keep a copy of it.

  • Birth date you enter during setup. It is used on your device to calculate your daily fortune and is never sent to us or to third parties.
  • Your cat: the name you give it, the cat you chose, today's outfit, hunger and feeding status.
  • App history: comfort cards you received, the date of your last visit.
  • Settings: language, notification on/off and times, background music and sound effects.
  • Reward counters: how many treats (Churu) and extra outfit changes you used today, so daily limits work.

2. Location and weather

If you allow location permission, CATCHI requests your device's approximate location to show the local weather and air quality in your cat's room. The latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo (weather and air-quality services) only to fetch the current forecast. CATCHI does not save your location history, and does not share your location with advertisers.

Location is optional. If you do not allow it, every feature except local weather keeps working.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CATCHI shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to give your cat a treat or to change outfits again on the same day. The reward is given only after the ad is completed. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, completion).

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and—where you have given consent or where permitted by law—show personalized ads. CATCHI does not send your birth date, cat name, or location coordinates to Google.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your advertising choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, CATCHI shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings › Ad privacy settings (shown where this option applies). Until a required choice is made, no ads are requested.
  • iPhone / iPad: CATCHI uses the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you don't allow it, or the prompt is not shown, the IDFA is not used. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the menu name may differ by device).
  • You never have to watch an ad, and declining consent or tracking never limits the fortune or cat features. Declining an ad only means its optional reward is not given.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, CATCHI schedules local notifications on your device (for example a daily fortune reminder at the time you choose). No push server is used and notification content is not sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Information in section 1 stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove all of it at any time by uninstalling CATCHI or clearing the app's data in your device settings. Because we have no servers storing your data, there is no copy left with us. Information processed by Open-Meteo or Google is kept according to their own policies.

8. Children

CATCHI is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that involved personal information, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your information. Since CATCHI's data stays on your device, you can view and change most of it directly in the app, and delete it by uninstalling. For advertising data held by Google, use the choices in section 4 or Google's tools. CATCHI does not sell your personal information.

10. Security

Information is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and network requests to Open-Meteo and Google use encrypted HTTPS connections.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date above will be updated, and important changes will be announced in the app.
